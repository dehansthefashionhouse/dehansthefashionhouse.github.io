Crafted with Intention

At DEHANS The Fashion House, we create bespoke men's traditional and casual garments with careful attention to fit, finishing and thoughtful design.

Our process begins by understanding the person who will wear each garment, from measurements and design preferences through to final finishing.

DEHANS Fashion Academy

DEHANS Fashion Academy is the fashion education arm of DEHANS, focusing on practical training in garment measurement, construction, sewing techniques and finishing.

The Fashion House serves bespoke tailoring and alteration clients; the Academy focuses on fashion learning.
